The World

Cogheim, Year 2526

A compressed disc world bound by a wall of ice. No safe borders. No static empires. Every nation on Cogheim is a Strider — a fortress-city the size of a district, walking, rolling, or grinding across the open disc. Your territory is wherever your engines take you, and your rivals are always moving too.
