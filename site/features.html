What You Will Do in Cogheim

A plain list of what is going into the game, each one marked by how far along it is. Shown in engine means we have watched it run. In build means it is being made now. In design means it is written down and not yet built.

Your City

Shown in engine

Live inside a city that moves

A Strider is a home, a workshop, a town square and a war machine, and you can walk around inside it while it travels. We have shown a player walking the inside of the Drifter under way and the outside of the Grindwalker while it moves. Crowds aboard are not proven yet.

In build

Five Strider classes

Blackjack, Grindwalker, Vector, Juggernaut and Scavenger. Each one moves differently, is laid out differently and suits a different kind of crew. See the class pages for a build log on each.

In build

One Helmsman at the wheel

A Strider is driven by one qualified Helmsman, a licence earned through research, the way you would earn a ticket for heavy equipment. Driving stays simple. Your instruments tell you what is damaged.

In build

The bridge war table

On the Grindwalker bridge, a map set into the floor shows the ground for kilometres around the ship and slides as the ship moves. A projector overhead throws holograms of what is in range: other Striders, a Colossus, an Auger. The commander plots a course there and the whole bridge sees it.

In design

Quarters and decorating

Homes live in the interior of your Strider, where you can build and decorate with Cogworks. You can also raise a house of your own out in the open world.

People

In design

Syndicates

The Strider is the Syndicate. Joining means boarding it. Founding one takes eleven people standing together. Read the Syndicate page.

In build

A named cast that knows each other

More than thirty named characters with their own voices, posts and history. Meet the first of them.

In design

Two guides, your choice

Suzette and Michael both look after you from the day you wake up. You pick who you go to, and they stay in touch for months of play, not just the opening hours.

In design

A world that remembers

The Syndicate Memory Engine keeps a record of what your crew has done, so a Strider builds a history other players can read. It is bounded and explainable, and it never keeps your private chat.

In design

Habits

A set of everyday tasks that are not quests. What you choose to do, day after day, shapes the kind of person your character becomes.

In design

Companions

Some of the named cast can be asked to come with you on a quest.

Making Things

In build

Cogworks

The building kit. Walls, stairs, catwalks and whole rooms from modular pieces. See Cogworks.

In design

Crafting and a carried pack

Workbenches by trade, a pack you actually carry, and materials that come from the ground you cross. A Strider gathers from the trees and nodes it rolls over, like a mower collecting clippings.

In design

Research at the Academies

New skills and machines are learned, not bought. Research rooms at the Academies are where a crew earns them, and quests can open research nobody had before.

In build

Music halls and playable instruments

Instruments you can pick up and play, a hall with a stage, and a showman who waves you up. The first hall and its instruments are being built for the Grindwalker deck.

In build

Radio

Stations you tune in while you work or travel, with their own hosts and music made for the game.

Travel

In build

Vehicles for air, land and water

Small craft that launch from your Strider: the Slipstreamer you learn to fly on the Drifter, heavier aircraft, land runners, boats and a submarine. Models for several are built. Flight and driving are still being tuned.

In design

Iron Frames

An open exoskeleton you climb into and sit inside, with your face showing. It rolls on powered skates and plants its heels to brace.

In build

Monuments

Tall stone towers with a market at their feet, and the only fast travel in the game. Find one to open it. A monument can carry you, your crew and your vehicle to another monument, or home.

In design

MAXIOM

Your own instrument: map, resources, crew log and comms, read through a lens and on the war table instead of a floating overlay. Menus are still there where a menu is simply easier.

Fighting

In build

The Auger and the Colossus

A kilometre of machine under the ground and a walking fortress above it. See the Threats page.

In design

A main weapon that takes a crew

The biggest gun on a Strider cannot be fired by one person. World shaping power takes cooperation, and the game is designed around people dropping out, time zones and bad actors.

In design

Gauntlets

Forty enemy runs across foot, vehicle, air and water, built for groups. Everyone gathers their own rewards.

In design

War is a choice

A Syndicate has to take on Martial standing before it can fight other players. A crew that never does can still arm itself against the things that hunt the disc.

The World

In build

One connected world

A single landmass ringed by ocean and walled by ice, built as real geology with forests, wetlands, lakes and coast. Take the long look.

Shown in engine

A sky everybody shares

Weather and time of day are the same for every player in the same place. Snow that remembers where it fell has been shown in engine. Read the weather post.

In design

A language you can learn

Einwechtëran is a real, learnable language with its own script. You will find it carved on monuments and old machines. It names things, warns and measures. It never explains itself.

In design

Harmonics

The closest thing Cogheim has to magic, taught as a discipline: like science, but magic. You learn it at the Academy.

In build

Wildlife

Birds, fish and herds that live on the disc whether you are watching or not.

Fair Play

In design

Zero pay to win

Real money never buys power. The paid currency is for looks only. Nothing that makes you stronger, faster or richer in the game can be bought.

In design

Readable by everyone

Subtitles on every line, sound cues that are also shown, and no information carried by colour alone.

Read this the right way: a feature on this list is a thing we are building, not a promise of a date. Scale figures you see around the site, such as Striders per server or crew sizes, are design targets and have not been reached or tested. The live tracker shows what has actually been proven.
