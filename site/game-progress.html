Read This Before The Numbers

Two Things Worth Knowing About These Bars

The Novels bar reads high mostly because the five-book Quintet is already finished — that's real, but it's the story, not the game. The game-side work of turning that story into playable systems is what's still ahead, and it's tracked separately in the bar below. The Memory bar reads low partly because that pillar's work has only been broken into a handful of large items so far, not because it's nearly untouched — a thin breakdown reads as a thin bar even when the underlying scope is real.

The Road, In Plain Language

What's Being Built Now, And What's Still Ahead

Now

One Strider, walkable, under way, with a player inside. That's the proof the whole game is possible, and it's the thing every current session is working toward.

Next

Feature-complete for the core loop — the Drifter tutorial, Meridian, Iron Frame exoskeletons, and RFA netcode all working together at load.

Later

Content-complete — all five Strider classes, the economy, Syndicates, live events, and 6–10 playable regions.

Eventually

Full live service — 35 regions, the complete roster, ongoing live operations.

No dates appear on this page on purpose. None of the above is scheduled — it's the honest shape of the work, in order.
