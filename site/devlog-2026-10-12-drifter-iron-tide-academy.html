The Drifter Ran the Disc, the Iron-Tide Surfaced, and the Academy Went Under

Seven days, three new things to look at, and one test we have been building toward since August. Here is what moved this week, with every claim labelled by how far it actually got.

How to read the labels: Seen in play ran in the editor's play mode. Compiled the code builds and loads, not yet tried in play. In Unreal the asset is in the engine. Blender only modelled, not yet in the engine. Design direction decided, not built. Concept reference art.

01The bar for the vertical slice

On 8 October we set the bar for the vertical slice in one line: the Drifter has to be drivable on the disc. Not dressed, not finished. Drivable, with a player able to walk it while it moves.

On 12 October it happened. Seen in play Inside a test session on the full disc map, the Drifter went through Slow, Half and Full. At Full, 257 metres from the start, the founder left the helm and walked the deck for about ninety seconds while the hull kept running under him. The automated checks for the moving hull netcode passed 73 of 73, and the server correction that pulls a walker back into line after a rejected move fired three times and recovered each time.

What this is not. It is one test session on one machine. It is not a multiplayer test and it says nothing yet about scale. The player snagged on a door on the bow walkway, the frame cost of the new hull was not measured, and the hull's own speed was read from the engine order rather than logged. We have not formally recorded the slice as passed. One session is the start of the evidence, not the end of it.

02Introducing the Iron-Tide

The heavy water vehicle is a steam submarine, thirty metres of riveted dark iron with a ram at the bow and lit portholes down both flanks. It is the only craft besides the Iron Frame that can reach the lore sites on the sea floor. This week it went from parts to an assembled model Blender only with its collision and mount points set up, and the founder settled how it fights Design direction: a saw built from the spines on its back, torpedoes that drop from the belly, and vertical missile tubes along each side.

03The Scavenger, in progress

The Scavenger is being rebuilt as an articulated train of capsule sections joined by accordion bellows, with a working salvage yard on top. A new Syndicate starts with three sections and earns up to five through research. It went through nine blockout versions this week, from a smaller chisel nose to tracks moved out into their own wells, a chairlift down its length and an aerodrome on the centre section. Blender only None of it is in the engine yet, and several parts still clash.

See the Scavenger blockout

04The Academy goes under the sea

Meridian Maritime Academy is where players study. It sits in shallow sea with floors above the waterline and four below it, wrapped around a central Well with a lift column running the full height. This week its interior went into Unreal In Unreal: more than a thousand Gothic kit pieces, wall lanterns, benches, glowing seams in the floor and a doorway for every discipline room. The lift code compiled Compiled. Nobody has walked the whole interior in play yet, and the two lowest floors still have no walls.

05Drifter v13: tracks, ladder, interior

The Drifter's rear running gear was rebuilt: five links wide, with a sprocket and an idler on every row Blender. The stern ladder now reaches out 205 centimetres as you approach and the rear door opens as you climb Compiled; neither has been tried with the Drifter moving. Inside, 19 new props replaced 42 old ones and the hull was placed on the disc map In Unreal.

06Grindwalker v20 on deck

At the start of the week the Grindwalker deck was laid out as a city: streets of stacked row homes, hangars and landing pads on the port side, and polished copper caps on the towers. These are editor captures of version 20 In Unreal.

07A first bestiary

The disc is getting wildlife: birds, land animals and sea life, each one a familiar animal reworked in iron, brass and boiler plate. Twenty two birds were rigged and brought into a test level in Unreal In Unreal. One wingbeat loop has been seen in the editor; none have flown in play yet. Nine sea creatures were imported before a graphics driver crash stopped the batch, and the rest are waiting. Names and sizes are design proposals and can still change.

08Something under the ground

One threat is being designed this week that we want to show early, because it is not the kind you fight. The Auger is a burrowing machine you cannot kill. You outrun it or you outwit it. Concept In design Everything about it can still change.
