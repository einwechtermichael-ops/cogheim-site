Meridian Maritime Academy

Every skill a player learns, they learn somewhere. Meridian Maritime Academy is that somewhere: a school standing in shallow sea, with part of it above the water and part of it under.

How to read the labels: Seen in play ran in the editor's play mode. Compiled the code builds and loads, not yet tried in play. In Unreal the asset is in the engine. Blender only modelled, not yet in the engine. Design direction decided, not built. Concept reference art.

The shape of it

The Academy is built around a central shaft we call the Well. Up the middle of the Well runs the Spindle, a column of open iron lift cars that carries you from the dry floors at the top to the wet floors at the bottom. The floors ring the Well, and every discipline has its own room off the ring.

The main floor is the Rose Hall. Design direction Its floor carries an inlaid compass rose of black and white marble, brass and worn wood, with a single feather at its centre. Thin lit lines run out from the centre and pulse toward each discipline's room, so from the gallery above you can see the whole school laid out in light.

Below the waterline

Four floors sit under the sea. Design direction The glass on them is fogged with sea growth, clear in the middle and faded at the edges, so the ocean outside feels close without feeling like an aquarium. On the third wet floor a ring of pods hangs off the main floor, joined by six sided tunnels, and one of them, the Deep Observatory, has a hatch in the floor down to a glass room in open water. The bottom floor holds the Iron Frame dive lock, the only way out at that depth.

Research rooms for twelve

Research is done in teams. Each research room is built for up to twelve players. When a team starts to enter, a thirty second clock begins, and one floor panel turns up for every member who makes it in. Arrive late and you miss it. There is no invisible wall; you are free to run around the room. Seen in play The timer and panels have been tested for teams of one and six.

A school, and a town

The Academy is not only classrooms. The plan includes a crafting floor with a foundry, a quay market and trade depot, a living floor and civic rooms Design direction. Its outer deck sits about two metres above the sea so boats can tie up, and submarines dock at their own station at the end of long piers: four berths under a barrel vault, with sea gates at each end shaped like arched cemetery gates. The whole Academy runs as its own space you load into, so it can be as detailed as it needs to be.

Where it stands

  • In Unreal The interior shell is placed and textured. This week added more than a thousand Gothic kit pieces, 138 wall lanterns, 72 lamp posts, 30 benches, glowing seams in the floor and doorways for every discipline room.
  • Compiled The code for the Spindle lift cars builds and loads. The cars themselves are not placed yet.
  • In Unreal The submarine station kit and the first exterior pieces are imported but not placed.
  • Open Nobody has walked the whole interior in play. The two lowest floors have no walls yet, and many kit pieces still need proper collision.
