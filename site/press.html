About COGHEIM

COGHEIM is a steampunk 4X MMO where the cities move. Set on a compressed disc world in Year 2526, players command Striders — massive mobile fortress-cities — across five classes, each with its own heritage, power system, and doctrine. Territory is never owned, only held: in COGHEIM, you can't capture a city, you have to catch it first.

COGHEIM is developed by Limitless Game Makers LLC, a founder-led independent studio. 30% of the studio's net proceeds support Solitude Shore, its non-profit arm.

About Limitless Game Makers

Limitless Game Makers LLC is a founder-led independent studio. Founder Michael “Boom” Einwechter works construction by day and builds COGHEIM at night — a production philosophy of lean costs, production-ready systems, and relentless iteration. The studio credits HealingUS Communities for community support, and commits 30% of net proceeds to Solitude Shore, its non-profit arm.
