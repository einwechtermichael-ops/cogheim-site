Iron-Tide

Most of the disc is ground you can see. The Iron-Tide is for the part you cannot. A steam submarine built to go where the Striders cannot follow, and to come back up with what it found.

What it is

The Iron-Tide is the heavy vessel of the water roster, alongside the light Tide-Runner and the medium Waterhammer, and it is the only one of the three that goes under. A riveted dark iron hull shaped like a sturgeon, with a ram spur at the bow, lit portholes down both flanks and a lamp at the nose. You pilot it in third person.

It matters for one reason above the others. Some of the lore sites on the disc are on the sea floor. Besides the Iron Frame, the Iron-Tide is the only way to reach them.

Built to ram

There is no tall smokestack on the Iron-Tide, on purpose. A stack would be torn off the first time it rammed under a hull. The brass banding around the hull is deliberate too. Brass is a rare accent everywhere else in Cogheim. On this boat it was allowed to stay, because it looks right.

How it fights Design direction

These decisions were made this week and are planned rather than built. Numbers can still move.

The spine saw

The sturgeon spines along its back are a weapon. They run back and forth like a chainsaw blade and only cut on contact, so you have to bring the boat in close. Run it too long and it heats up, then needs time to cool.

Torpedoes and missiles

Torpedoes drop from long panels in the belly that fold down to release them. The round openings along each side are not windows but vertical missile tubes. The current plan is twelve weapon slots, filled in pairs: up to four torpedoes and eight missiles, with the spine saw taking four slots of its own.

Earned, not bought

A new Iron-Tide starts with two open slots. Research opens them two at a time, and the spine saw becomes available once six are open. How you fire is earned the same way: first from the hip, then locking a target, then a torpedo you steer yourself with the camera riding behind it until it hits or runs out. Slots, firing modes and the saw all live in the Iron-Tide's own questline and research tree. Nothing about it is for sale.

Where it stands

  • Blender only The hull was assembled this week from generated parts, with a new propeller, portholes, ballast blisters, keel fin and hatch. Collision shapes and mount points are set up.
  • Blender only The spine saw is a separate moving part. The torpedo doors, a torpedo, a missile, a missile cap and a torpedo cradle were modelled and fitted.
  • Open It is not in Unreal yet. Depth rating, speed and endurance are not set, and the missile bays are still hollow.
