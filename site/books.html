The Cogheim Quintet

Five Books. One World.

The novel series that built COGHEIM's canon from the ground up — written by series creator Michael Einwechter. All five volumes are complete.

The Series

The Full Account, Start to Finish

I

The Great Grind

A hundred and fifty years after the Suture, a sealed manifest is waiting for a name it already knows. Kael has no memory of who he was before the Drifter found him — only the sense that the disc itself has been waiting for him to arrive.

Complete
II

The Suture Seams

Kael has a name now, a vessel, and a crew of fourteen moving toward a primary node the Directorate has spent eight months failing to reach. The Craton-Scanning Array speaks a language older than the compact itself — and what it says is designed, not accidental.

Complete
III

The Iron Meridian

The Iron Veil has held forty years of documented neutrality. One morning, the compass at its center points inward for the first time. Sara Vuitton built this institution and has never once used the suspension clause written into its founding compact — until now.

Complete
IV

The Iron Butterfly

The Einwechter line is older than anyone admitted. At the threshold, GAIA-9 speaks two names instead of one — and everything the compact called neutral starts to look like a forty-year positioning strategy.

Complete
V

The Full Account

Every runner, every record, every sealed drawer opens at once. The joining is prepared on pre-compact paper. The Oasis requires both lineages present, together, for the first time in the disc's recorded history.
