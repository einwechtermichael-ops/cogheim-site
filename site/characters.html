World of Cogheim · The Cast

Who You Will Meet

Cogheim has a named cast of well over thirty. This is the first group going into the game: the crew of the Drifter, where every player starts, and a few of the people waiting beyond it.

Aboard the Drifter

You wake up on this ship with no idea how you got there. These are the people who were already living on it. They talk to each other when you are not in the room, most of them have history, and each of them teaches you something you need before you leave.

SR

Suzette Rayo-Amor

Captain of the Drifter

The first voice you hear in Cogheim. She runs the ship that found you, and she is one of the two guides you can choose to lean on.

M

Michael

Commands alongside Suzette

Your other guide. Where Suzette teaches you the ship, Michael teaches adventure: the long quests, the side roads and the places with big rewards.

RS

Rori Spark-Maw

Master mechanic

Keeps the Drifter running. Later in the game she runs the Colossus watch and teaches gunnery at the Academy, because the one job taught her the other.

J

Joel

Drifter crew

Big brother to Rori in everything but blood. Most of the crew knew each other long before you woke up aboard.

JR

Jason Root-Water

The well digger

A survivor of Ocre-Post. He knows what it takes to find water where nobody else can.

JS

Jess St. Pete

Archivist

Keeper of things worth keeping. Your first delivery is a sealed case she puts in your hands.

AF

Astin Fog-Eye

Drifter crew

One of the first faces you learn aboard. His head is on the bench now.

M

Morgann

Aeronaut

She comes aboard late and for one reason: the Slipstreamer on the roof does not fly, and she can fix that. She is the one who teaches you to fly.

A

Axiom

The ship's unit

Not a person, and nobody aboard treats it like furniture either. The Axiom unit is the last word before you leave the Drifter.

Beyond the Drifter

The disc is bigger than one ship. A few of the people you will find out there:

JD

Joey Dawn-Well

First Officer at the Iron Veil, and a teacher

First Officer is his post. Teaching is his calling. His lessons are about how to live well, and his questline asks what you think you are worth.

N

Nathaniel

Showman of the music halls

Top hat, centre stage, and the man who waves you up to play. He is also the voice of your survival guide.

PT

Penny Truex

Chief Science Officer

Works the Applied Synthesis research room at the Meridian Maritime Academy. You meet her early, at its door.

K

Kayla

Relic hunter and leg walker

Tracks old Vector wrecks for original parts and repairs the legs that carry the class. She greets new Vector crews.

PE

Professor Evan House

Teaches Harmonics

Very tall, always stooping, a mandolin on his back. He teaches the Academy hall the way a good teacher does, standing among the students.

SV

Sara Vuitton

Keeper of the Veil

Holds the Iron Veil. Compass gear pin, aviator glasses, and decades of neutrality behind her.

How the Cast Is Being Built

Every named character gets a real head, a real body and a real voice. The crew of the Drifter comes first because you see them close up, talking to you, from the first minute.

  • Ten body types. Five male and five female builds, signed off, so the cast does not share one silhouette.
  • Faces one at a time. Each head is built on its own and then fitted for speech. Several were redone from scratch when the first pass did not look like the person.
  • Voices. Every character is cast separately. Subtitles carry every line.

Honest status: this roster is where the cast stands today. Posts can still move as the game is built, and portraits go up here as each head is finished. No character on this page is in a playable build yet.
