FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything people ask about COGHEIM — the world, the Striders, the economy, and how to get in early. Straight answers, no spin.

What is COGHEIM?

COGHEIM is a steampunk 4X MMO set on a compressed disc world in Year 2526. Instead of building a static city, you command a Strider — a massive moving fortress-city. Territory is never safe, because every empire is in motion.

What are Striders?

Striders are the massive moving fortress-cities you command in COGHEIM. Each is a living city with its own crew, decks, and power core, and it walks across the world rather than sitting on a fixed plot.

What are the Strider classes?

There are five Strider classes: Bastion, Juggernaut, Scavenger, Vector, and Grindwalker. Each has a distinct heritage, power system, movement profile, and playstyle.

How is COGHEIM different from other strategy MMOs?

In most strategy MMOs your base is a fixed point on a shared map. In COGHEIM the whole city moves — so you can't capture a city, you have to catch it first. The chase comes before the war.

Is COGHEIM pay-to-win?

No. Real money never buys gameplay power in COGHEIM. Premium currency covers cosmetics, time-skips, the Battle Pass, and crew slots only, and no currency converts into any other.

What platforms will COGHEIM be on?

COGHEIM is PC-first, with console planned to follow. A mobile companion app handles timers, repairs, resource collection, and march ETAs — it complements the game rather than replacing it.

When does COGHEIM release?

COGHEIM is in active development. No release date has been announced. Enlist on the website to get first word on alpha invites, dev logs, and Strider reveals.

Will COGHEIM be free-to-play?

The pricing model has not been announced. Whatever it ends up being, real money never buys power — premium spend is limited to cosmetics, time-skips, the Battle Pass, and crew slots.

What is the MAXIOM?

The MAXIOM is your wrist-mounted device. It keeps your empire's map, resources, build queue, crew log, and comms one glance away, on the move.

Can I walk around inside my Strider?

Yes. On PC your Strider has walkable interiors — command decks, crew quarters, and core systems you can move through — so your city is a place, not just a menu.

Who is developing COGHEIM?

COGHEIM is developed by Limitless Game Makers LLC, a founder-led independent studio in the USA. 30% of the studio's net proceeds support Solitude Shore, its non-profit arm.
