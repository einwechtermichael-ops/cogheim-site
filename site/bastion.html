What Bastion Is

Bastion is the fortress standard — the silhouette of the disc itself. Built on a Statue of Liberty hull, it runs deep districts beneath a Roof Aerodrome and holds command of the Iron Veil zone. It is not the fastest or the strangest hull on Cogheim. It is the one every other crew measures itself against, and the one a coalition forms behind when the disc turns hostile.

The anchor. The line everyone else forms on.
